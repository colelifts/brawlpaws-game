# BrawlPaws Combat VFX V2 — Built-in Image Generation Prompts

All sheets were generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. The supplied BrawlPaws gameplay and level-up images were used only as palette, brushwork, scale, and mood references. Production files were converted from flat chroma-key backgrounds to transparent PNGs.

Every prompt shared these constraints: premium hand-painted comic/anime game VFX; exactly six equal animation frames in a strict 3-column by 2-row grid; read left-to-right then top-to-bottom; crisp silhouette; controlled bloom; generous cell padding; no UI, text, labels, numbers, borders, dividers, frame overlap, cast shadows, or scenery; perfectly flat chroma green or magenta background.

## Spirit Blaster projectile

Six frames: charge spark, muzzle emergence, compact traveling bolt, stretched high-speed bolt, energized pulse, fading tail. Cyan-white plasma core, magenta trailing accents, dark navy edge definition. Horizontal left-to-right travel that rotates cleanly to any angle.

## Spirit Blaster impact

Six frames: pinpoint contact, sharp starburst, cyan-magenta plasma bloom, peak shattered-energy crest, fragment breakup, dissipating sparks. Centered radial hit with no travel trail.

## Hostile Spirit Arrow projectile

Six frames: spectral ignition, bow-release streak, compact travel, stretched high-speed arrow, unstable spirit flicker, fading tail. Icy cyan arrowhead, blue spectral feathers, ghost-smoke tail, indigo rim, hostile violet core.

## Hostile Spirit Arrow impact

Six frames: puncture spark, cyan flash, icy spectral burst, blue-violet shatter, ghost-fragment breakup, fading motes.

## Foxfire burn loop

Six seamless flicker phases of a compact low ring of foxfire around a target's feet. White-yellow cores, orange flame bodies, purple spirit-fire edges and motes. Empty center so the enemy remains visible.

## Tidal water impact and Wet application

Six water-only frames: narrow splash, curling arc, broad cyan impact crown, peak foamy burst, loose droplets, lingering wet drips and blue motes. No character or creature. Empty center for target placement.

## Jade Brawler claw slash

Six frames: red anticipation streaks, first claw emerging, fast triple-claw sweep, peak hot-pink arc, torn ember fragments, fading scratch trails. Scarlet, hot pink, orange-white cores; no gore.

## Jadebreaker hammer slam

Six isometric ground frames: contact spark, orange fractures, circular shockwave, stone-and-jade debris crown, collapsing dust ring, fading glowing cracks.

## Shock Paws strike

Six frames: violet charge spark, cyan contact, magenta electric paw burst, peak jagged star explosion, residual branching arcs, fading electric motes.

## Shock Paws link

Six different natural horizontal lightning paths with consistent endpoints. White-hot core, cyan inner glow, magenta-violet branching arcs. Designed to stretch and rotate between targets.
