# Zap v1 production art

Generated with the repository's established BrawlPaws visual language and the supplied roster concept as the character reference.

- `zap-movement-key.png`: transparent-key 4×4 directional movement atlas; eight idle/facing poses followed by eight full-body running poses.
- `zap-fire-key.png`: transparent-key 4×4 directional firing atlas; eight anticipation poses followed by eight paired arc-caster releases.
- `zap-states-key.png`: transparent-key 4×2 authored state atlas; Undertow, Foxfire, Wild Heart, Shock Paws, hit, stun, defeat, and victory.
- `zap-portrait-key.png`: square roster/HUD portrait.
- `zap-arc-pulse-key.png`: transparent-key 3×2 charge, travel, and impact VFX atlas.

Character brief: a small athletic yellow rabbit Techie in fitted navy-blue and gold Japanese fantasy armor, long swept ears, white muzzle, cyan spirit-electric accents, and two compact forearm arc casters. Preserve readable silhouettes, consistent proportions, complete feet/ears/weapons, and no text or UI.

Production PNGs were derived with the imagegen chroma-key removal helper and are stored in `assets/characters/` and `assets/vfx/`.
