# Neon City v1 source prompts

All generated art was directed to match the established BrawlPaws painterly comic-anime look: high-detail 3/4 isometric composition, dark inked silhouettes, saturated cyan/magenta/gold lighting, readable combat silhouettes, no text, and no UI.

- Seven 16:9 production backgrounds: Neon Rain Gate, Circuit Market, Hologram Arcade, Skyrail Shrine, Data Lotus Gardens, Shogun Tower, and the Neon Shogun Core. Each keeps the center and lower-middle floor open for gameplay while dense moving-light architecture frames the arena.
- Enemy combat atlas: Circuit Jackal cyber-swordsman, Pulsewing Crow ranged drone-monk, and Chromeback Gorilla reactor brute, arranged as three columns with idle poses above and clearly distinct full-body attacks below on a flat chroma-key background.
- Enemy movement atlas: the same three enemies in two planted stride poses, consistent proportions and weapon design, on chroma key.
- Daikyo Oni combat atlas: giant white-tiger cyber shogun in a 3×2 layout showing idle, windup, sweep, slam, channel, and enraged poses on chroma key.
- Daikyo Oni movement atlas: two heavy planted locomotion poses with consistent armor, reactor core, and silhouette on chroma key.
- Neon combat VFX atlas: six isolated effects in a 3×2 layout—cyber claw slash, pulse projectile, giant oni sweep, firewall warning sigil, electric impact, and kernel-collapse explosion—on chroma key.

Production versions were matted to transparency with edge feathering and spill cleanup. The original keyed outputs are retained here for traceability.
