# BrawlPaws Ability VFX — Generation Prompts

These source sheets were made with OpenAI's built-in image generation using the BrawlPaws gameplay and level-up concept images as visual references. Each prompt requests an exact 3-column by 2-row animation atlas. Production PNGs in `../../vfx/` have their chroma backgrounds removed.

## Shared art direction

Production-ready 2D game VFX sprite sheet for a premium, fast isometric action roguelite. Bold hand-painted comic/anime shapes, crisp silhouettes, bright emissive cores, controlled bloom, energetic tapered motion, and no UI, characters, text, labels, borders, dividers, scenery, or cast shadows. Exactly six equally sized frames arranged in a strict 3x2 grid, read left-to-right then top-to-bottom. Keep every effect centered with generous safe margins and no frame overlap.

## Tidal Slash

Create a six-frame animation of one cyan-blue water slash: compressed anticipation, opening crescent, fast release, broad impact arc, foamy breakup, and small sparkling dissipation. The slash travels horizontally from left to right and must rotate cleanly to any gameplay angle. Use a flat solid chroma green `#00ff00` background with no gradients or texture.

## Foxfire Bolt

Create a six-frame animation of one magical foxfire projectile: ignition spark, small fox-head flame forming, launch stretch, high-speed flight, impact flare, and ember dissipation. Use a bright orange-white core with a vivid purple/magenta flame tail. The bolt travels horizontally from left to right and must rotate cleanly to any gameplay angle. Use a flat solid chroma green `#00ff00` background with no gradients or texture.

## Wild Heart

Create a six-frame stationary healing cast: green seed-light, leaf spiral, luminous heart forming, full heart-and-leaf bloom, expanding restorative aura, and soft sustained heart glow. Keep the heart readable at small scale and make the effect centered around the caster without covering the character. Use emerald, lime, and pale mint light on a flat solid chroma magenta `#ff00ff` background with no gradients or texture.

## Spirit Inferno

Create a six-frame centered stationary ultimate eruption: purple summoning sparks, spectral fox crest forming, orange-and-violet flame surge, wide peak inferno crescent, collapsing spirit fire, and drifting embers. The effect should feel powerful without filling the whole screen or hiding the player. Use a flat solid chroma green `#00ff00` background with no gradients or texture.
