# Spirit Lantern Village component kit

Generated as three independent production atlases for the BrawlPaws in-browser map maker. The assets intentionally contain no labels, UI, characters, baked scene background, or collision information. Visible art and gameplay geometry remain separate.

## Terrain atlas

Top-down painterly hand-inked action-roguelite terrain atlas, exact 4-by-4 grid of sixteen square seamless floor swatches, each cell isolated and filling its cell edge-to-edge: warm sandstone plaza, cracked sandstone, engraved shrine stone, dark wet stone, packed earth, mossy earth, gravel, leaf soil, jade grass, flowering grass, bamboo deck, dark wood floor, shallow water, deep water, glowing spirit path stone, volcanic shrine stone. Cohesive Japanese fantasy village palette, rich material detail, no objects, no text, no borders or gutters, game-ready texture tiles.

## Architecture atlas

Top-down three-quarter isometric hand-painted BrawlPaws Japanese fantasy village architecture asset atlas, exact 4-by-3 grid with twelve independent centered structures: shrine hall, forge workshop, dojo pavilion, merchant shop, spirit portal torii, moon shrine, relic altar pavilion, mission board shelter, stone bridge, wooden footbridge, roofed village gate, lantern house. Dark navy roof tiles, inked silhouettes, magenta/cyan/orange spirit lantern accents, readable entrances and ground contact, flat chroma green backdrop for alpha extraction, no shadows baked into the background, no labels, no people, generous transparent-safe padding.

## Props atlas

Top-down three-quarter isometric hand-painted BrawlPaws Japanese fantasy village prop atlas, exact 5-by-4 grid with twenty centered props: cherry tree, jade maple, bamboo cluster, flowering shrub, spirit tree, mossy boulder, rock cluster, stone lantern, cyan lantern, red lantern, bamboo fence, stone wall, market cart, shrine drum, well, torii signpost, barrels and crates, spirit brazier, blank banner, lily pond. Crisp silhouettes, high material detail, cohesive inked action-roguelite style, flat chroma green backdrop for alpha extraction, no labels, no characters.
