# Jade Grove Animated Environment — Built-in Image Generation Prompts

These sheets use the BrawlPaws gameplay images as visual references and the same strict 3x2, six-frame atlas format.

## Spirit wisp

A seamless loop of one compact cyan-white teardrop spirit with a turquoise aura and violet tail, softly breathing, tilting, stretching, and returning to its first pose. Flat magenta chroma background; no face, character, scenery, text, or shadow.

## Lantern flame

A seamless magical flame loop with a white-hot base, gold-orange body, and tiny magenta spirit sparks. Natural lean, split, settle, and return phases. Flat green chroma background; flame only.

## Water ripple

An isometric pond-ripple loop: cyan glint, first ellipse, double ripple, broad luminous ripple, fragmented ring, fading motes. Flat magenta chroma background; ripple only.
